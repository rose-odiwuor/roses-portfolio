## Brand & Style
The brand personality is analytical, authoritative, modern, and rigorously structured. Tailored for Rose Odiwuor, a Data Analyst based in Nairobi, Kenya, the identity communicates absolute precision and intellectual clarity without sacrificing warmth. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a refined, architectural minimalism. Generous negative space balances high-density data visualizations, metric scorecards, and case summaries. Layouts rely on flat surfaces, strict square geometry, and high typographic contrast to present data science expertise with enterprise credibility. Structural boundaries are razor-sharp with no heavy rounding, evoking the precision of analytical tools and tabular grids.

## Colors
The palette balances high-contrast functional color with measured, warm tints:

- **Primary (`#DC2626`)**: Bold Red. Used for critical interactive focal points: primary CTA actions, active navigation states, section eyebrows, inline text links, and key metric callout icons.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`)**: Slate Navy. Provides ground for social links, terminal accents, and technical tags, offering balanced weight against the primary red.
- **Tertiary (`#FDF2C4`)**: Soft Warm Cream. Exclusively grounds the final anchor surfaces—the contact block and portfolio footer—creating a tactile close to the single-page narrative.
- **Neutral Surface & Text**:
  - Primary Text: `#111827` (Near-black) for optimal readability and strict contrast.
  - Secondary Text: `#4B5563` (Muted Slate) for captions, table headers, and body metadata.
  - Border Lines: `#E5E7EB` (Thin Light Gray) defining all card boundaries and grid tracks.
  - Base Canvas: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Alternate Section Canvas: `#FDF7F7` (Very light pink-tinted off-white) to demarcate alternating content bands such as Experience and Case Studies.
  - Accent Containers / Chip Badges: `#FCE8E8` (Pale Pink) paired strictly with `#DC2626` text for skill chips, status badges, and icon backplates.

## Typography
Typographic structure relies entirely on `Inter` to enforce crispness, systematic hierarchy, and technical neutrality.

- **Headlines & Eyebrows**: Section headers use heavy font weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking to achieve an editorial, commanding voice. Eyebrows are always forced uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.1em`) rendered in primary `#DC2626`.
- **Body & Data**: Content uses natural tracking with generous line-heights (1.5–1.55x) in `#111827` to ensure reading ease. Secondary data context drops to `#4B5563`.
- **Metrics**: Numeric outcomes and quantitative impact stats render in bold tabular figures to emphasize business impact cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column fixed-max grid centered on desktop (max width: `1200px`) with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. On mobile screen sizes (<768px), the layout compresses to a 4-column flow with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins.

Section vertical rhythm alternates between dense information arrays (`space-xl`) and breathing room across narrative chapter boundaries (`space-3xl`). Padding inside data cards follows an asymmetric formula: larger horizontal boundaries (`space-xl`) to establish stable edges, paired with balanced vertical padding (`space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is strictly flat and architectural, relying on planar layering and perimeter boundaries rather than prominent elevation drops:

- **Border Hierarchy**: Cards and structural modules use a precise `1px solid #E5E7EB` border.
- **Ambient Shadow**: Elevation is minimal. Interactive cards use an ultra-diffused shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive State Depth**: On desktop hover, cards retain their flat plane without vertical movement; instead, the border shifts to `#DC2626` or `#D1D5DB` with a subtle elevation bloom: `0px 8px 24px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06)`.
- **Backing Alternation**: Visual division is achieved by shifting background colors between pure `#FFFFFF`, `#FDF7F7`, and the footer block `#FDF2C4`, eliminating the need for elevated surface planes.

## Shapes
Sharp square corners (`0px` border-radius) define all elements across the system. Buttons, metric cards, skill badges, form fields, and image frames must not include rounded corners. 

This strict geometric language reflects tabular structures, grid lines, and data spreadsheets, distinguishing the system from consumer apps and framing the analyst’s work as rigorous, objective, and precise.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rect (`0px` radius), solid `#DC2626` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-md`). Hover state transitions to `#B91C1C`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#DC2626`.
- **Secondary (Navy / Social)**: Solid `#1E293B` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#0F172A`. Used for GitHub, LinkedIn, and external code repository links.
- **Tertiary / Outline**: White background, `1px solid #E5E7EB`, `#111827` text. On hover: border color changes to `#111827`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Analytical Skill Tag**: Sharp rectangular tag with `#FCE8E8` background and `#DC2626` text (`label-sm`). Padding: `4px 8px`. No border.
- **Tool / Tech Tag**: Light slate `#F1F5F9` background with `#1E293B` text for general technologies (e.g., Python, SQL, Tableau).

### Icon Tiles
- Sharp 40x40px or 48x48px square containers filled with `#FCE8E8`. Icons are centered, 20px/24px stroke-based SVGs colored strictly in `#DC2626`.

### Cards
- **Project & Case Study Cards**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline, `0px` radius. Padding: `24px`. Displays an eyebrow, bold title, quantitative metric outcome, concise narrative, and categorical tags.
- **Metric Highlight Cards**: Feature top-bordered emphasis (`2px solid #DC2626`), white surface, housing bold metric figures (`code-metric`) in `#111827` over secondary labels in `#4B5563`.

### Navigation Bar
- Sticky, `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E5E7EB` bottom border. Contains name mark in `#111827` (`weight: 800`), standard navigation links in `#4B5563`, and active navigation links indicated by `#DC2626` text accompanied by an underline or square dot marker.

### Input Fields
- Flat background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #D1D5DB`, `0px` corners, `12px 16px` padding. Active and focused state transitions the border to `1px solid #DC2626` with no soft outer glow. Placeholder text in `#9CA3AF`.

### Contact Section & Footer
- Background switches entirely to `#FDF2C4`. Enclosed elements sit on white or transparent cards with thin `#E5E7EB` or `#E4D8A5` borders, anchored by primary red interactive accents and dark navy social elements.
